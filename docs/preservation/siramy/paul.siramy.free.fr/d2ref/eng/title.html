NUMBER OF ACTS PASSED

 This value determines a lot of thing : the acts you can access with waypoints, the difficulty level where you can play, and your honorific title (this last one is also affected by the mode in wich you play : Normal or Hardcore).

At the begining of the game, the value is initialized at 0. Each time you passed an act (by killing a boss), this value increased by 1.

When it reach 4   (5 in expansion)   (Last Boss killed 1 time) :

  • you can play in Nightmare difficulty level
  • your honorific title reach 1st level (Sir / Dame, Count / Countess, Slayer, Destroyer)
When it reache 8   (10 in expansion)   (Last Boss killed 2 times) :
  • you can play in Hell difficulty level
  • your honorific title reach the 2nd level (Lord / Lady, Duke / Duchess, Champion, Conqueror)
When it reache 12   (15 in expansion)   (Last Boss killed 3 times) :
  • your honorific title reache its last level (Baron / Baroness, King / Queen, Patriarch / Matriarch, Guardian)
